## Full description (4000 chars max)

Remember that little window popping up at the bottom of your screen, the
login sound, changing your status ten times a day, nudges that shook the
whole screen? K-ssenger brings that feeling back, updated for today.

No infinite feed. No algorithm deciding who you should see. Just you and the
people you actually want to talk to.

**What you get:**
- Private, end-to-end encrypted conversations — not even we can read them.
- Group chats for your crew, like the good old contact lists.
- A little nod to the nudges and emoticons we all loved.
- A personal status, because we've always liked saying what's on our mind.
- A simple, fast app, no bloat.

K-ssenger is built for people who want honest messaging: no ads inside your
chats, no selling your data, no infinite scroll designed to keep you hooked.
Just talking to your real friends.

**Closed beta** — we're building this with our first users. Your feedback
matters, we read all of it.
